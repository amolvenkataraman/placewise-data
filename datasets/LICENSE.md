curated by the placewise project and licensed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The placewise code is under its own licence; this notice covers the data in `datasets/` and the
copies generated from it (`src/placewise/constants/countries/regions.py` and the like, and the
`regions` layer of the data packs).

You may share and adapt them for any purpose, including commercially, as long as you give
appropriate credit, link to the licence and say whether you made changes. Credit line:

> placewise curated datasets, CC BY 4.0

Facts the datasets record (which counties a metro area takes in, what a planning body's region
is) come from the public sources each entry cites in its `definition`. The polygons in the packs
are unions of official boundaries, so they also carry the boundary source's own attribution
(see ATTRIBUTION.md, generated with `placewise attribution`).
